# Sauna Flip Timer

A calm split-flap timer for your sauna and rest rounds, for iPhone.

Set your sauna time, rest time and rounds, turn the phone sideways, and relax. The Dynamic Island and the lock
screen keep counting. No sounds, no vibrations, no notifications – nothing to disturb the quiet.
