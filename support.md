# Support – Sauna Flip Timer

Questions or problems? Write to [saunaflip@s88s.me](mailto:saunaflip@s88s.me).

## How do I start a session?

Set your sauna time, rest time and rounds, then turn your phone sideways – or tap "Turn phone sideways to
start". After a 10-second shuffle the countdown starts.

## How do I pause?

Tap the screen, then "Pause". Tap the screen again to resume. While paused you can add or remove 30 seconds,
skip to the next phase, or end the session.

## Why no sound or vibration?

On purpose: the app is meant to stay quiet in the sauna. The screen flashes at each phase change, and the
Dynamic Island and lock screen show where you are.

## The Dynamic Island shows only a small ring

When another app's Live Activity is active at the same time, iOS shows Sauna Flip only in its smallest form: a
ring that fills over the session. The full timer comes back when the other activity ends.

## How do I log a session to Apple Health?

Tap "Log to Apple Health" on the "Session complete" screen, or later in History. If iOS asks, allow Sauna Flip
to write workouts (Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Sauna Flip).

## How do I delete a session?

In History, swipe left on a session (or long-press it) and choose Delete. If the session was logged to Apple
Health, you can delete the workout there as well.
