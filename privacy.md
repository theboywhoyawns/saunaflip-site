# Privacy Policy – Sauna Flip Timer

*Last updated: 4 November 2026*

Sauna Flip Timer ("the app") is published by DYNAMEYES s.r.o.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting service. It
makes no network connections of its own.

## What stays on your iPhone

- Your session settings and your session history (date, duration, number of rounds, pauses) are stored only on
  your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
- While a session runs, the app shows a Live Activity on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. This is
  handled by iOS on your device.

## Apple Health

If you choose "Log to Apple Health", the app saves the session as a Mind & Body workout in Apple Health on your
device. It asks for permission to write workouts only and reads no Health data. When you delete a session and
choose to delete it in Health too, the app removes the workout it saved. Health data is managed by Apple under
Apple's privacy terms; we never receive it.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
